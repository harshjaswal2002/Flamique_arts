GOOD TO KNOW

The little details.

How do I care for my candle?

Always use a stable, heat-resistant surface, keep away from children, pets and draughts, and never leave a lit candle unattended. Follow the care instructions supplied with your specific candle.

Can I order a gift or a custom piece?

Gift sets and custom pieces are part of our plans. Customisation options, timelines and contact details will be added before we begin accepting orders.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery regions, dispatch times and shipping policies are being finalised. The preview checkout illustrates the shopping experience; orders are not accepted yet.

What about payments and returns?

Online payments and offline payment options will be confirmed at launch. Return eligibility, cancellation rules and refund timelines will be published before orders open.
