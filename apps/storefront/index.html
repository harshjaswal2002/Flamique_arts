04 / THE PRACTICAL PART

Before you order.

How do I care for my candle?

Always use a stable, heat-resistant surface, keep away from children, pets and draughts, and never leave a lit candle unattended. Follow the care instructions supplied with your specific candle.

Can I order a gift or a custom piece?

Looking for a thoughtful gift or a custom piece? Message us on WhatsApp at +91 78378 89616 to discuss ideas, availability and timelines.

Where do you deliver?

We deliver across Chandigarh Tricity and nearby areas. Delivery is ₹50 for orders below ₹200 and free for orders of ₹200 or more. WhatsApp us to confirm coverage for nearby areas. For nearby-area deliveries, confirm coverage with us before ordering.

What about payments and returns?

We offer cash on delivery. If your order arrives damaged, contact us to arrange a return, refund or replacement after review. Our support team will guide you through the next steps.
